How to play

  1. Read the activities on your bingo card.
  2. Complete an activity that resonates with you.
  3. Tap or click each square as you go.
  4. Complete 5 consecutive squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to achieve BINGO.
  5. Once you complete a line, download your card and share it with Group HR if needed.
  6. Try to complete all the squares by Friday, 23 October 2026.
