# Mental Health Bingo

**Small actions. Meaningful moments.**

A digital bingo card for **Aitken Spence • Mental Health Month 2026**. Staff complete simple wellbeing activities, tick them off and download their completed card.

## How to play

1. Read the activities on your bingo card.
2. Complete an activity that resonates with you.
3. Tap or click each square as you go.
4. Complete 5 squares in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally to achieve **BINGO**.
5. Once you complete a line, download your card and share it with Group HR if needed.
6. Try to complete all the squares by **Friday, 23 October 2026**.

## Downloading your card

Click **Download completed card**. A preview of your card will appear and the image will download automatically.

If the download does not start:

- Click **Download PNG**, or
- Click **Copy image** and paste it (Ctrl+V) into an email or Teams chat, or
- Right-click the image and select **Save image as** (on mobile, press and hold the image and select **Save image**).

For best results, use **Microsoft Edge** or **Google Chrome**.

## Privacy

- No data is collected or sent anywhere.
- Your ticked squares and name are saved only in your own browser on your device.
- Clearing your browser data or using a different device will reset your card.

## Updating the page

Upload a new version of `index.html` to this repository. The page link stays the same.
